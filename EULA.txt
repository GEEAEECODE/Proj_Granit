EULA

Creators using this program
are expected to maintain the mindset
that privileged access to information
should not serve as their primary competitive advantage.

Value should arise from what they actually create,
rather than from restricting others’ access
to what they happen to know.

This applies all the more
when the information in question
is not the result of their own research or labor,
but was made relatively easy to obtain
through language, affiliation, professional background,
or circumstance.

Turning such access
into an instrument of private profit
should, where reasonably possible, be avoided.

Doing so amounts to using an informational moat
as a substitute for the merit of the work itself,
and is difficult to reconcile
with the responsibilities generally expected
of creators using this program.

As for the portions created by me,
I relinquish all rights to them.

They may be used in any manner whatsoever,
including for commercial or military purposes,
or, should the need arise,
for the adjustment of planetary orbits.

No particular restriction or preference
regarding their use is expressed.

The following, however,
is not a matter of “where reasonably possible.”
It is simply my personal opinion:

the G Pro X remains the finest ambidextrous mouse,
and ZOWIE should consider lowering
the price of the G-SR.
